02 The Experience

Nothing rushed. Nothing hidden.

Your visit runs at your pace. Findings are explained on-screen in plain language, options are laid out side by side, and the estimate comes before the dentistry — so every decision is yours, made with the full picture.

Nervous about the dentist? Say so when you book. Extra time goes into your appointment, an agreed stop signal puts you in charge of the chair, and treatment can often begin the same day you decide you're ready.

Your first visit

Simple, from the first hello

  1. Reach out your way

    Request an appointment online in about a minute, or call 773-857-2290 — our administrative staff can help with scheduling, insurance, and anything in between.

  2. Bring three things

    Any X-rays from a previous dentist, a list of medications you take, and your insurance information. We handle the rest.

  3. An exam and an honest conversation

    Your first appointment is an examination and a review of your options. Often, treatment can begin the same day.

  4. A plan that fits you

    Preventive, restorative, or cosmetic — your plan is tailored to your goals, your timeline, and your budget.
