Book it in a minute

The calendar below is the practice's own — real openings, on our own system. Pick a time and it's held for you on the spot; a person confirms it. Prefer the phone? 773-857-2290 always works.

How it works

From open slot to reserved chair

  1. Pick your time

    The calendar shows the practice's real openings. The moment you choose one, it's held for you — nobody else can take it.

  2. We confirm

    The front desk reaches out to confirm the appointment and answer anything up front, including insurance questions.

  3. Come as you are

    Bring prior X-rays if you have them, your medication list, and your insurance details — that's it.

Common questions

Before you book

I have a toothache right now. Should I book online?

Call instead: 773-857-2290. Emergencies are triaged by phone so we can see you as soon as possible and tell you what to do in the meantime.

After you call, our emergency page covers first-aid steps for common situations.

Can I book for my child?

Yes — Dr. Maskatiya treats patients of all ages. Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

What if I haven't seen a dentist in years?

Then you're exactly who this practice was designed for. No lectures — just an exam, clear findings, and options laid out without judgment.
