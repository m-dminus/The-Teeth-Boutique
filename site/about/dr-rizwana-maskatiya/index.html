The Dentist

Dr. Rizwana Maskatiya, DDS

A calm demeanor, a warm approach, and more than a decade of clinical dentistry — for patients of every age.

Background

Trained in Chicago, practicing for Chicago

Dr. Maskatiya earned her degree in Dental Surgery from the University of Illinois at Chicago and has spent more than ten years in clinical practice, providing preventive, restorative, and cosmetic care to patients of all ages.

She maintains membership in the American Dental Association and the Chicago Dental Society, and stays current through continuing education in the techniques and technology that move dentistry forward.

Photograph — Dr. Maskatiya, portrait

Her calm demeanor and warm approach help ease the most anxious of patients.

The philosophy of the practice

How she practices

Every treatment plan at The Teeth Boutique is customized — built around the patient's objectives, not a production schedule. Dr. Maskatiya explains findings in plain language, presents every option straight (including the option to wait), and uses contemporary technology — digital imaging, high-power microscopes, laser dentistry — in the service of comfort and precision.

For patients who feel nervous in a dental chair, this practice was designed with you in mind. Tell us. Nothing about your visit will be hurried, and every step will be explained before it happens.

Away from the practice, Dr. Maskatiya spends her time with family, plays cricket, and travels.

Meet her in person.

Your first appointment is an exam and an honest conversation about your options — often with treatment available the same day.
