The Practice

A boutique, in the honest sense

Not a chain, not a franchise, not a rotating cast of providers. One dentist, one North Side address, and care for every member of the family.

The word “boutique” gets used loosely. Here it means something specific: dentistry practiced at a personal scale. The Teeth Boutique is a single-location practice on Irving Park Road in North Center, led by Dr. Rizwana Maskatiya, DDS — the same dentist at every visit, for every member of the family.

It also means fewer handoffs. Retainers and night guards are made in our own lab, so a replacement or an adjustment happens here, quickly, checked by the dentist who examined you rather than shipped off and waited on.

And it means the experience itself: appointments that start on time and aren't rushed, plain-language explanations, prices on the table before treatment, and a calm chairside manner that anxious patients notice within minutes.

What we stand on

Three commitments

Personal

Care for all ages from one dentist who knows your history — not whoever is on the schedule that day.

Precise

High-power microscopes, digital imaging, and electric handpieces — precision instruments, used routinely.

Unhurried

Appointments that start on time, explanations in plain language, and an estimate before any treatment begins.

A University of Illinois at Chicago graduate with more than ten years of clinical experience, Dr. Maskatiya treats patients of all ages across preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry. She is a member of the American Dental Association and the Chicago Dental Society.
