The Instruments

Precision you can feel

Technology is not the point — comfort and accuracy are. These are the instruments we use every day to get there.

In the operatory

Six tools of the practice

High-Power Microscopes

Magnification that enhances the precision of examination and treatment — details the naked eye simply cannot resolve.

Electric Handpieces

For the most precise aspects of restorative work: consistent torque, smoother preparation, quieter operation.

Digital Imaging

Modern digital imaging throughout the office — with radiation exposure time roughly half that of traditional film.

Digital X-Rays

Images reviewed together on a monitor — larger, clearer, and easier to understand than film held to a light.

Laser Dentistry

Light-energy treatment — one of dentistry's most notable modern advances — applied where it improves comfort and outcomes.

Intraoral Cameras

A camera just a few millimeters long shows you exactly what we see, so decisions are made with your eyes open.

Beyond the operatory

Precision that continues after the impression

Retainers, night guards, and orthodontic appliances are made in the practice's own lab and fitted by the dentist who designed them — so the accuracy of the records taken at the chair carries through to the appliance in your hand, without a wait for a distant laboratory.
