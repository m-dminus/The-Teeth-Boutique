The Practice

Made here, for you

Your retainer, night guard, or orthodontic appliance is made in the practice's own lab — which means faster turnaround, fittings by the dentist who designed it, and repairs that don't leave the building.

What it means for you

Fewer handoffs, faster fittings

At most offices, an appliance is sent out to a distant laboratory and you wait for it to come back; if it doesn't fit, it goes out again. Here the appliance is made by the practice that examined you, to Dr. Maskatiya's prescription, and she is the one who seats it, checks it, and adjusts it.

For you that means shorter waits, fewer return trips, and one accountable person from the first impression to the final fit. Lost your retainer? Cracked a night guard? Call us — a replacement is made here, not ordered from somewhere else.

How it works

From examination to fitting

  1. Examination & records

    Dr. Maskatiya examines you and captures the records your case needs — the design starts with your anatomy, not a generic template.

  2. Designed by your dentist

    The appliance is planned by the dentist responsible for your outcome, not by a stranger at a distant facility.

  3. Made here

    The appliance is made in the practice's own lab, to that prescription, under our own quality check.

  4. Fitted and adjusted by the same dentist

    The dentist who designed it seats it, checks it, and adjusts it — and is the one you see if it ever needs attention.

The person who examined you is the person who fits your appliance.
