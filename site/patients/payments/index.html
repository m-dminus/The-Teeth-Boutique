Patient Center

Settle up, simply

Check, credit card, in person, or by phone — paying a balance shouldn't require a manual.

Payment is due at the time of service, and the practice accepts payment by check and credit card, with cash discounts available. For balances that arrive after insurance processes — most insurers respond within four to six weeks — you can settle by phone at 773-857-2290 or at the front desk at your next visit. The Patient Portal shows your balance and payment history under your own account.

If a balance is larger than comfortable, don't sit with it silently: flexible payment plans and CareCredit exist for exactly this, and our administrative staff arranges them without ceremony.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Can I pay over the phone?

Yes — call 773-857-2290 or 773-857-2280 during office hours and the front desk will take care of it.

I got an insurance statement I don't understand. Who do I call?

Us, first — explanation-of-benefits documents are our native language. We'll tell you what it means and whether anything is actually owed.

A question about a balance?

Call 773-857-2290 — clarity is free.
