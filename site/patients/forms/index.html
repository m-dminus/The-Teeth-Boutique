Patient Center

Paperwork, minimized

Three fillable forms cover registration, records transfer, and referrals — download what you need below, or skip it all and do everything at the desk. Our forms, on our site; nothing routes through an outside form service.

How to use them

Each form is a fillable PDF: complete it on your computer or phone (or print and use a pen), save it, and bring it to your visit or hand it to the front desk. To protect your health information, please don't send completed forms by regular email — records release forms can also go by fax to 773-857-2260.

The registration form matters clinically: medications and conditions like diabetes, high blood pressure, or anticoagulant therapy shape how care is safely planned. Unsure about a field? Leave it blank — our administrative staff completes forms with patients every day, insurance boxes included.

Prefer paper? Arrive a few minutes early and everything will be ready at the desk. The practice's Notice of Privacy Practices is available at the office.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How early should I arrive for a first visit?

If your forms are done, arrive at your appointment time. Completing them here? A few minutes early is plenty — the set is short.

What if I don't know my insurance details?

Bring the card and we'll take it from there — verifying coverage and explaining what your plan does is part of what our front desk does every day.

Can you request my records from my old dentist for me?

Yes — complete the records release form naming your previous office and we'll handle the request. Prior X-rays often save you from needing new ones.
