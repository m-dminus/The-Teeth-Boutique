The practice

About The Teeth Boutique

What are your hours?

Monday and Wednesday 9:30 am – 5:00 pm, Saturday 9:00 am – 3:00 pm — with Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, and Sunday available by appointment. Call 773-857-2290 and we'll find a time that genuinely works.

Where are you located?

1933 W Irving Park Rd, Ste 1, Chicago, IL 60613 — on Irving Park Road in North Center, a short walk from the Irving Park Brown Line, serving Lakeview, Lincoln Park, Avondale, Uptown, and the surrounding North Side.

What makes this practice 'boutique'?

Scale: one dentist who knows your history, one location, no assembly-line scheduling, and appointments that are never rushed. Even your retainer or night guard is made and fitted here rather than sent away — care and craft under the same roof.

Do you treat children?

Yes — patients of all ages, and Dr. Maskatiya's calm manner is particularly loved by young and nervous patients. Under-18s must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Money

Insurance & cost

Which insurance do you work with?

Plans including Delta Dental, Guardian, Humana, MetLife, Ameritas, Cigna Dental, Renaissance Dental, Aetna, Careington, and Dental Plans. As a courtesy, we bill your insurance and track claims for you. Not listed? Call — the answer is one phone call away.

What if I don't have insurance?

You'll get clear pricing before treatment, cash discounts, and access to flexible payment plans and CareCredit. Uninsured doesn't mean unwelcome — it means we plan the numbers with you up front.

Will I know costs before treatment?

Yes — estimates come before dentistry, always. Payment is due at time of service, and financing options exist for larger plans.

Care

Visits & treatment

I'm terrified of dentists. Honestly, how will this go?

Slowly, gently, and with everything explained before it happens. Dr. Maskatiya's calm demeanor and warm approach ease even the most anxious patients — it's the trait her patients mention most. Tell us when you book; the visit will be paced for you.

What happens at the first appointment?

An examination and a review of your treatment options — often with treatment available the same day if you want it. Bring prior X-rays if you have them, your medication list, and your insurance details.

What do I do in a dental emergency?

Call 773-857-2290 first — we triage by phone and tell you exactly what to do. For a knocked-out adult tooth, minutes matter: handle it by the crown, keep it moist (milk is ideal), and get here within the hour.

Can old dental work be redone?

Frequently, yes — aging crowns, worn fillings, and discolored bonding are everyday restorative work. Start with an exam so the plan addresses causes, not just appearances.
