The first visit

Four steps, no surprises

  1. Book your way

    Online in about a minute, or by phone — Monday and Wednesday 9:30–5, Saturday 9–3, with other days available by appointment.

  2. Gather three things

    Any X-rays from a previous dentist (if you have them), a list of medications you're presently taking, and your dental insurance information.

  3. The exam and conversation

    Your initial appointment is an examination and review of your treatment options — explained on-screen, in plain language.

  4. Often, same-day treatment

    Usually, treatment can be done or started the same day as the consultation. Complex cases may need a follow-up visit for a deeper evaluation.

Worth telling us before treatment

Please alert the office if you have a medical condition that may be of concern prior to treatment — diabetes, high blood pressure, artificial heart valves or joints, rheumatic fever, and the like — or if you're on medication such as heart medications, aspirin, or anticoagulant therapy. None of these usually prevents dental care; they simply shape how we plan it safely.

Two practical notes: patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian, and if you'd rather we request your X-rays from your previous provider, we can — or new ones can be taken at your visit if needed.

Good to know

First-visit questions

I'm anxious about dentists. What should I do?

Say so when you book — it changes how we pace everything. Dr. Maskatiya's calm, unhurried approach is this practice's signature, and anxious patients are treated as the rule here, not the exception.

Will I get pushed into treatment?

No. You'll get findings, options, and real prices — including the option to phase treatment or wait where that's clinically reasonable. The plan is yours to choose.

Do you see children?

Yes — all ages, from first teeth to dentures. Families often book together.
