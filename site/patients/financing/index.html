Patient Center

Good dentistry, payable sensibly

Cost should shape the plan's timing — never whether you get proper care. Here's how patients make treatment affordable.

The ground rule is candor: you'll know what treatment costs before it begins, and payment is due at the time of service. Around that, the practice offers real flexibility — flexible payment plans on a no-interest or low-interest basis, discounts for cash payment, and payment by check or credit card.

For larger treatment plans, CareCredit — a healthcare financing credit line accepted here — lets patients spread costs over time through promotional financing options. Applying is quick, and our front desk can walk you through how it works with your specific treatment plan.

The quiet advantage of a practice like this one: treatment can usually be phased. Urgent work first, important work next, elective refinement when it suits your budget — a sequenced plan is often the difference between 'someday' and 'started.'

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — flexible plans on a no-interest or low-interest basis, arranged with our administrative staff alongside your treatment plan.

What is CareCredit, exactly?

A credit line designed for health expenses, usable for dental care here. It offers promotional financing options and a quick application — ask us and we'll explain exactly how it would apply to your plan.

Is there a discount for paying cash?

Yes — cash discounts are offered. Ask the front desk for specifics when your treatment plan is priced.

Talk numbers before treatment.

Estimates first, options second, dentistry when you're comfortable.
