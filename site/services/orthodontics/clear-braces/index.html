Orthodontics

Fixed control, quiet look

Clear braces do what braces have always done well — precise, continuous control of tooth movement — without announcing themselves in every photo.

Clear braces work on the same principle as traditional braces: brackets bonded to the teeth, connected by a wire that guides movement continuously. The difference is cosmetic — tooth-colored or translucent brackets that blend with your smile instead of contrasting against it.

Because they're fixed, clear braces don't depend on wear discipline the way removable aligners do, and they handle certain complex movements — rotations, significant bite corrections — that trays manage less predictably. For some cases, they're simply the better instrument.

Which tool fits your case is a clinical judgment, not a preference quiz. Dr. Maskatiya will spell out what each option can and cannot do for your particular bite.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Clear braces or Invisalign — which is better?

Neither, universally. Aligners win on removability and near-invisibility; fixed braces win on continuous control and independence from wear-time discipline. The right answer depends on the movements your case requires.

Do clear braces stain?

Modern bracket materials resist staining well, though elastic components can discolor with heavy coffee, tea, or curry — one reason to keep up with hygiene visits during treatment.
