Good to know

Orthodontics, answered

Am I too old for orthodontic treatment?

No. Healthy teeth can be moved at nearly any age — adults are a large share of modern orthodontic patients, and discreet options like aligners and clear braces were designed with them in mind.

How do I know which option fits me?

That's what the consultation is for. Dr. Maskatiya examines your bite, discusses what you want to change, and lays out the true trade-offs of each route — timeline, visibility, discipline required, and cost.

Start with a straight answer.

An orthodontic consultation tells you what's possible, how long it takes, and what it costs — before you commit to anything.
