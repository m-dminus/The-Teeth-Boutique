Orthodontics · Retainers

Keep what you earned

Teeth drift for life — a retainer is how you make an orthodontic result permanent. Ours are made here and fitted by the dentist who straightened your teeth.

After braces or aligners, the fibers and bone around your teeth need time to stabilize in their new positions — and without a retainer, teeth begin migrating back toward where they started. Retention isn't an upsell at the end of orthodontics; it's the half of treatment that protects the other half.

Retainers here are made in the practice's own lab: the dentist who planned your alignment designs your retention, the retainer is made to that prescription, and the same dentist fits it and adjusts it at the chair. No waiting on a shipment, and no second trip if a refinement is needed.

Lost or cracked a retainer from us? Call. A replacement is made here from your records, so it is usually ready sooner than one ordered from an outside laboratory — and the fit is checked by the person who knows your case.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How long do I have to wear a retainer?

Think of retention as long-term, tapering with time per your dentist's guidance. The blunt truth: teeth that aren't retained tend to move. Wear schedules vary by case, so follow the one prescribed for yours.

I had braces years ago and my retainer is long gone. Can you help?

Yes — start with an exam. If your alignment is still worth holding, a new retainer can be made; if drift has already happened, Dr. Maskatiya will show you where things stand and what your options are.
