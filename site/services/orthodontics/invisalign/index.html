Orthodontics

Invisalign, supervised properly

A series of clear, removable aligners moves your teeth in small, planned steps — without brackets, wires, or a mouth full of metal.

Invisalign uses a sequence of custom clear aligners, each worn for a set period, to move teeth gradually into position. The trays are nearly invisible in conversation, and because they're removable, you eat, brush, and floss exactly as you always have — two of the practical reasons adults so often choose them over traditional braces.

What matters as much as the product is the supervision. Your case is planned and monitored by Dr. Maskatiya, who checks that movement is tracking to plan and adjusts course when biology has other ideas. Aligner therapy is dentistry, not a subscription box.

Invisalign suits many — not all — cases of crowding, spacing, and bite misalignment. The consultation settles whether you're one of them, or whether another route would serve you better.

What to expect

How Invisalign works here

  1. Consultation & records

    An exam and imaging establish whether aligners can achieve what you want — and what the realistic timeline looks like.

  2. Your series is planned

    Dr. Maskatiya maps the movement stage by stage, so you can preview where your smile is going before you begin.

  3. Wear, switch, progress

    You wear each aligner set as directed, switching on schedule, with periodic checks to confirm everything is tracking.

  4. Retain the result

    When treatment ends, a retainer — made here, fitted by the same dentist — protects the investment for the long run.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How long does Invisalign take?

It depends entirely on your case — minor crowding may resolve in months, complex movement takes longer. You'll get a realistic, case-specific estimate at your consultation, not a slogan.

Does it hurt?

Expect pressure for a day or two with each new aligner — that's the feeling of teeth being asked to move. Most people describe it as soreness, not pain, and it fades as each stage settles.

How many hours a day do I wear them?

Aligners only work while they're on your teeth — plan on wearing them essentially all day and night, removing them for meals and brushing. Wear discipline is the single biggest factor patients control.
