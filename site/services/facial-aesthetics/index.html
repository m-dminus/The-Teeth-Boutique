Facial Aesthetics

Finishing touches, clinically done

The frame matters as much as the picture. Botox and dermal fillers, administered by a clinician who works around facial anatomy every day.

Dentists are, by training, anatomists of the face — muscles of expression, nerve pathways, symmetry, and proportion are daily working territory. That's why facial aesthetics in a dental setting makes more sense than it might first sound: the injector understands the structures under the skin, and the setting holds clinical standards.

The philosophy here is restraint. The goal of both Botox and fillers, done well, is a rested and refreshed version of your own face — never a different one.
