Facial Aesthetics

Soften the lines, keep the face

Botox relaxes the specific muscles whose repeated contractions crease the skin — softening expression lines while your expressions remain yours.

Botox is a purified protein that temporarily quiets targeted muscle activity. Applied with precision to the muscles that fold the forehead, knit the brow, or crinkle the eye corners, it softens the lines those movements have etched — the result reads as rested, not frozen, when dosing respects the anatomy.

Treatment is quick — a series of small injections in a short visit — with effects appearing over the following days and typically lasting a few months before gradually fading. Because the effect is temporary, so is any decision: an inherently low-commitment way to try a refreshed look.

A candid consultation comes first: your goals, your medical history, and a plain read on what Botox will and won't do for the lines that bother you. Static folds that persist without expression are often filler territory instead — and we'll say so.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Will I look frozen?

Not with conservative, anatomy-respecting dosing — the aim is softened lines with living expression. Restraint is the house style.

How long does it last?

Typically a few months, varying by individual and area treated. Effects fade gradually rather than ending abruptly.
