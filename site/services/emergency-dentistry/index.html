Emergency Dentistry

In pain? Call first.

Dental emergencies are triaged by phone so we can see you as soon as possible — and tell you exactly what to do in the meantime.

Call 773-857-2290Request an appointment

A true dental emergency — trauma, uncontrolled bleeding, swelling that affects breathing or swallowing — belongs in an emergency room. For everything else that feels urgent (and truly is), your first call is your dentist: severe toothache, a broken or knocked-out tooth, a lost crown or filling, swelling near a tooth.

When you call, describe what happened and what you're feeling. We'll tell you how soon you need to be seen, what to do right now, and what to avoid. Acting quickly matters most with knocked-out teeth, where minutes genuinely change outcomes.

Emergency questions

I'm not sure it's an emergency. Should I still call?

Yes. Describing your symptoms by phone costs nothing and gets you a professional read on urgency. Pain that wakes you at night, swelling, or a tooth newly sensitive to pressure deserve prompt attention.

What if it happens on a day you're not regularly open?

Call 773-857-2290 regardless — the practice runs by-appointment days beyond its regular Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday hours, and the phone is how urgent needs get slotted.
