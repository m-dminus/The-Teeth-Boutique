Why here

Fit is looked after for years, not handed over once

Dentures live and die by fit, and fit changes as gums and bone change. We treat every denture as a precision appliance that will need real attention over its life — not a product delivered once and forgotten.

That's why the denture service here runs deep: relines when the fit loosens, repairs done properly rather than patched, soft liners for tender ridges, and scheduled exams that catch problems while they're small.
