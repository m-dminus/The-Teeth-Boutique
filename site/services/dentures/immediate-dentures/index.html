Dentures

Never a day without a smile

An immediate denture is made before your extractions — and placed the same day — so healing happens in private, not in public.

When teeth must come out, the question patients fear asking is simple: will I have to walk around without teeth? With an immediate denture, no. The appliance is fabricated in advance from impressions taken while your teeth are still present, then seated at the same visit as the extractions.

The trade-off, stated up front: gums and bone change shape as they heal, so an immediate denture's fit evolves over the first months. Plan on adjustments — and eventually a reline or a refined final denture once healing stabilizes. We build that reality into the plan from day one, so nothing about the process surprises you.

What to expect

How immediates work

  1. Impressions before extraction

    Records are taken while your teeth are still in place, and the denture is fabricated in advance.

  2. Extraction day = denture day

    Teeth are removed and your immediate denture is seated at the same visit.

  3. Healing & refinement

    As tissues heal and reshape over the following months, the fit is adjusted — culminating in a reline or final denture.
