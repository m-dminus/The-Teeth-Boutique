Cosmetic

The plan behind the pretty

Great smiles aren't bought procedure by procedure — they're designed once, then built in the right order.

Smile design is the consultation that should precede any serious cosmetic work. It starts with your complaint in your words — the photo you avoid, the tooth your tongue finds, the shade that's drifted — and proceeds through a working inventory: shade, shape, proportion, alignment, gum line, and how your smile actually moves when you talk and laugh.

From that comes a sequenced plan. Order matters in cosmetic dentistry: alignment before veneers can mean fewer veneers; whitening before bonding means the bonding matches the brighter you; gum health before everything, always. A designed sequence routinely costs less than the same procedures bought impulsively — and reads better in the mirror, because it was composed as a whole.

The plan is priced line by line, staged to your timeline and budget, and includes the version we'd recommend if you were family — which is sometimes the least expensive one on the page.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Is a smile design consultation a sales pitch?

It's the opposite — a design exists so you buy the right dentistry in the right order, or confidently choose less than you feared you needed. The recommendation you'd give family is the house standard.

What should I bring?

Photos help: your smile now from a few angles, and any photo — of you in earlier years, or anyone — that shows what you're drawn to. Specific complaints beat vague goals, and we'll translate either.
