Good to know

Preventive care, answered

How often should I really come in?

For most healthy mouths, professional cleaning and examination twice a year is the working rhythm. Gum disease history, heavy tartar formation, or certain medical conditions can shorten that interval — you'll get a recommendation based on your mouth, not a template.

I haven't been to a dentist in years. Will I get a lecture?

No. You'll get an exam, straight findings, and a plan that starts where you are. Coming back is the hard part — we make everything after that easy.

Due — or overdue?

Either way, the next step is the same: book an exam and know where you stand.
