Your teeth fight at night

Grinding and clenching happen while you sleep, with forces chewing never approaches. A custom guard takes the hit so your enamel doesn't.

Bruxism — nighttime grinding and clenching — is something most sufferers discover secondhand: a partner hears it, a dentist spots the flattened edges, or mornings arrive with a tight jaw and a dull headache. The forces involved are remarkable, and over years they chip enamel, crack restorations, wear teeth visibly shorter, and aggravate the jaw joint.

A custom night guard is a precisely fitted appliance that sits over the teeth and absorbs those forces — protecting enamel from enamel. Unlike boil-and-bite drugstore guards, a custom guard is made from records of your actual bite: slimmer, more secure, and comfortable enough that you'll actually wear it, which is the entire game.

Your guard is made in the practice's own lab, so its fit and follow-up stay close at hand: checked at your regular exams, adjusted when your bite changes, and replaced here if it cracks or goes missing.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I know if I grind?

Clues: morning jaw tightness or headaches, tooth edges that look flattened, chipped restorations, a partner who hears it. The wear pattern on your teeth tells the story clearly at an exam.

Why not a drugstore guard?

Bulky, loose, and often abandoned in a nightstand within weeks — and an ill-fitting guard can subtly disturb your bite. A custom guard fits your actual teeth, which is why it gets worn.
