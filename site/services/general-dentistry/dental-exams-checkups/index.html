General & Preventive

The exam is everything

Every good outcome in dentistry starts the same way: someone looked carefully, measured carefully, and explained clearly.

A checkup here is not a glance and a goodbye. Dr. Maskatiya examines your teeth for decay and fracture, your existing restorations for wear, your gums for the pocketing that signals periodontal disease, and the soft tissues of your mouth — including screening for abnormalities you'd never spot yourself.

Technology does the seeing where eyes can't: digital X-rays reveal decay between teeth and trouble beneath the gumline, high-power microscopes resolve detail invisible to the naked eye, and an intraoral camera puts what we see on a screen in front of you — so findings are shown, not just described.

Then comes the part patients remember: a plain-language conversation. What's healthy, what's watching-worthy, what needs treatment now, and what it costs — with your questions answered before anything is scheduled. Often, treatment can begin the same day if you want it to.

What to expect

A checkup, start to finish

  1. History & imaging

    We update your health history and take digital X-rays when they're due — at roughly half the exposure time of traditional film.

  2. The examination

    Teeth, gums, existing dental work, bite, and soft tissues — examined methodically, with magnification.

  3. Findings, on screen

    Intraoral camera images show you exactly what we see. No mystery, no take-my-word-for-it.

  4. Your plan

    Priorities, options, and costs laid out in plain terms — including when the right plan is simply 'watch and recheck.'

Good to know

Questions, answered

How long does a first exam take?

Plan for a full appointment — a proper first examination with records takes real time. Bring prior X-rays if you have them, a list of medications you take, and your insurance information.

Do you check for oral cancer?

Yes — examination of the soft tissues of the mouth is part of a thorough checkup. It's quick, painless, and one of the quietly important things a regular exam does.
