Implant Dentistry

The closest thing to growing it back

A dental implant replaces the whole tooth, root included — a fixed answer to a missing tooth that looks, works, and cleans like the real thing.

An implant is a small titanium post placed in the jaw, where it fuses with bone and becomes the foundation for a crown. Unlike a bridge, it doesn't lean on neighboring teeth; unlike a removable appliance, it doesn't come out at night. Done well and maintained, implants are the modern standard for replacing a missing tooth.

They also protect what you can't see: the jawbone under a missing tooth shrinks over time without a root to stimulate it. An implant keeps that bone working, which is part of why implant dentistry is about long-term architecture, not just filling a gap.

At The Teeth Boutique, your implant care stays in one place — examination, planning, restoration, and the maintenance visits afterward — with Dr. Maskatiya accountable for the outcome across all of it.

What to expect

The road to an implant

  1. Assessment

    An exam and imaging establish bone quality, gum health, and whether an implant is the right answer for your gap — or whether another restoration serves better.

  2. Placement & healing

    The implant is placed, then the bone integrates around it over a period of months — the quiet phase that makes the result permanent.

  3. The restoration

    A custom crown is attached to the integrated implant, matched to your neighboring teeth in shade and shape.

  4. Maintenance

    Implants can't decay, but the gums around them still need care — routine hygiene visits protect the investment for decades.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Does getting an implant hurt?

Placement is done under local anesthesia, and most patients report the recovery is milder than they feared — manageable soreness for a few days is typical. You'll get specific, case-level expectations for your case before anything is scheduled.

How long do implants last?

With good hygiene and regular checkups, implants routinely serve for decades — the implant itself often outlasts the crown on top of it. Longevity depends heavily on gum health, which is where maintenance visits earn their keep.

What if I'm missing several teeth?

Implants can anchor bridges — and even full dentures. Implant-retained dentures are one of the most life-changing options in modern dentistry, and this practice offers them alongside its full denture service.
