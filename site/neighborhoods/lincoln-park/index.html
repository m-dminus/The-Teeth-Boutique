Serving Lincoln Park

Boutique standards, without boutique attitude

Lincoln Park knows quality when it sees it. Our patients from the neighborhood make the short trip north for dentistry held to that standard.

Request an appointmentCall 773-857-2290

A practice named The Teeth Boutique had better deliver on the word, and for Lincoln Park patients that's precisely the draw: cosmetic dentistry planned like design work — veneers, bonding, whitening sequenced properly — alongside comprehensive care delivered by the same dentist at every visit, in appointments that never feel rushed.

The trip is easy from anywhere in the neighborhood — up Lincoln Avenue or Ashland to Irving Park Road — and worth it for the part you can't see on a map: one dentist who knows your history, appointments that start on time, and estimates before any treatment begins.
