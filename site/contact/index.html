Before you visit

Practical questions

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — new patients of all ages are welcome. Request an appointment online or call 773-857-2290.

What should I bring to a first visit?

Any X-rays from a previous dentist (we can also request them, or take new ones here), a list of medications you currently take, and your insurance information.

Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Do you take my insurance?

The practice works with plans including Delta Dental, Guardian, Humana, MetLife, Ameritas, Cigna Dental, Renaissance Dental, Aetna, Careington, and Dental Plans — and as a courtesy we bill your insurance and track claims for you. Call with your plan details and we'll verify your coverage before you're ever in the chair.
