Registered Dental Hygienist

Hygiene here is clinical work, not a conveyor belt — appointment lengths that respect the patient in the chair, and a doctor who reads your charting.

Download the application (PDF)Call 773-857-2290

The role

Preventive and periodontal care for patients of all ages: prophylaxis, scaling and root planing, charting, radiographs, sealants and fluoride, and honest home-care coaching patients can actually follow. Many patients arrive anxious — an unhurried chairside manner matters here as much as instrumentation.

Requirements: active Illinois RDH license in good standing and current CPR/BLS certification. Local anesthesia certification and experience with digital charting and intraoral imaging are each a plus — note them on the application.

How to apply

From download to delivered

  1. Download the application

    One fillable PDF covers every role — open it in any PDF reader, on a computer or a phone.

  2. Fill it in and save it

    Complete the fields, check the position you're applying for, and save the file with your name in the filename.

  3. Send it with your resume

    Bring both to the front desk at 1933 W Irving Park Rd, Ste 1 — or call 773-857-2290 and we'll arrange delivery by email.

  4. We review within the week

    Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. If your experience fits, we'll reach out to schedule a conversation.
