The practice

The Teeth Boutique is boutique by design: one location in North Center, one dentist, and a team that moves with intention. Patients are known by name, anxious patients are treated as the rule rather than the exception, and retainers and night guards are made in-house rather than sent out.

Work here if you believe dentistry is craft and hospitality at the same time. The schedule is posted, the standard is high, and the work is visible.

How to apply

From download to delivered

  1. Download the application

    One fillable PDF covers every role — open it in any PDF reader, on a computer or a phone.

  2. Fill it in and save it

    Complete the fields, check the position you're applying for, and save the file with your name in the filename.

  3. Send it with your resume

    Bring both to the front desk at 1933 W Irving Park Rd, Ste 1 — or call 773-857-2290 and we'll arrange delivery by email.

  4. We review within the week

    Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. If your experience fits, we'll reach out to schedule a conversation.

Good to know

Employment questions

Are roles full-time or part-time?

Either can be discussed — the application asks which you're looking for, and scheduling is settled in conversation.

What is the office schedule?

Patient hours are Monday and Wednesday 9:30 am – 5:00 pm and Saturday 9:00 am – 3:00 pm, with other days by appointment — team schedules are built around them.

Do you take applications when nothing is posted?

Yes. Applications are kept on file, and the practice hires for fit when the right person appears.

How do I apply?

Download the fillable application above, complete it, and bring it to the front desk with your resume — or call 773-857-2290 to arrange email delivery.
