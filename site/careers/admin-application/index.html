Administrative / Front Desk

The first voice patients hear and the person who makes the practical side painless — scheduling, insurance, and follow-through.

Download the application (PDF)Call 773-857-2290

The role

Scheduling and recall, phones and patient messages, insurance verification and claims follow-up, treatment-plan estimates, and payments — the administrative spine of a practice that promises patients clarity before dentistry.

Requirements: accuracy, discretion with patient information, and warmth under a busy phone. Dental front-desk experience and familiarity with practice-management software are each a plus; insurance-coordination experience is worth calling out on the application.

How to apply

From download to delivered

  1. Download the application

    One fillable PDF covers every role — open it in any PDF reader, on a computer or a phone.

  2. Fill it in and save it

    Complete the fields, check the position you're applying for, and save the file with your name in the filename.

  3. Send it with your resume

    Bring both to the front desk at 1933 W Irving Park Rd, Ste 1 — or call 773-857-2290 and we'll arrange delivery by email.

  4. We review within the week

    Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. If your experience fits, we'll reach out to schedule a conversation.
