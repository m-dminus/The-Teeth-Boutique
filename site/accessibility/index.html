Accessibility Statement

Care should be reachable — on this website and in our office.

The Teeth Boutique is committed to making its website usable by as many people as possible. This site was built with accessibility practices informed by the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1: semantic page structure and landmarks, text alternatives for meaningful images, sufficient color contrast, keyboard-navigable menus and controls, visible focus indicators, and motion that respects reduced-motion system preferences.

Accessibility is a practice, not a checkbox. If any part of this website is difficult for you to use — with a screen reader, by keyboard, at high zoom, or otherwise — please tell us: call 773-857-2290, and include the page and the difficulty if you can. We will work to address it and to provide the information you were seeking by another means in the meantime.

The same commitment applies in person. If you or a family member needs accommodation to receive care comfortably at our office, tell us when you book and we will plan for it.

Something on this site not working for you?

Call 773-857-2290 — we'll get you the information another way and fix the page.
